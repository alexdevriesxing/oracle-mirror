# M7.6 — Astrology & Lunar Expansion

## Purpose

This pass adds a standalone `/astrology` namespace around Oracle Mirror's existing Western Zodiac and Birth Chart experiences. The new layer is primarily educational/reference content plus one local Moon-phase calculator. It deliberately separates observable astronomical facts from symbolic astrological interpretation.

## Routes

The pass adds 43 canonical URLs:

- `/astrology`
- `/astrology/moon`
- `/astrology/moon/phases`
- eight `/astrology/moon/phases/:phase` pages
- `/astrology/zodiac`
- twelve `/astrology/zodiac/:sign` pages
- `/astrology/planets`
- ten `/astrology/planets/:body` pages
- `/astrology/retrogrades`
- five `/astrology/retrogrades/:planet` guides for Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn
- `/astrology/transits`
- `/astrology/birth-chart`

The legacy `/western-zodiac` and `/birth-chart` tools remain unchanged and are linked from the new guides.

## Moon calculation

`public/astrology-core.js` implements a transparent lightweight lunar model:

- average synodic month: `29.53058867` days;
- epoch: known new moon at 2000-01-06 18:14 UTC;
- requested calendar dates are evaluated at UTC noon to avoid local DST/date rollover problems;
- lunar age is normalized into one synodic cycle;
- illuminated fraction is approximated from phase angle;
- the continuous cycle is divided into eight conventional phase labels.

The UI labels the result **approximate** and explicitly states that exact lunar event times require a proper astronomical ephemeris because real lunar motion is non-uniform.

The calculator makes no feature API or Workers AI requests and persists no selected date.

## Zodiac reference

The 12 tropical signs include:

- conventional approximate calendar range;
- element;
- modality;
- traditional/modern ruler labels;
- original answer-first summary;
- strength and challenge copy.

Every sign page explains that fixed magazine-style dates are only approximations. Exact solar ingress varies by year and time, so births near a sign boundary require an ephemeris/birth-chart calculation.

## Planet reference

Ten bodies are covered: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. Each page has two explicitly separated sections:

1. **Astronomy** — physical/orbital context.
2. **Astrology** — the symbolic meaning assigned by Western astrological traditions.

The site does not present symbolic associations as demonstrated physical causal effects.

## Retrogrades and transits

Retrograde pages explain that planets do not literally reverse their orbit. Retrograde motion is an apparent reversal against the background stars generated by changing Earth/planet viewing geometry. Symbolic retrograde meanings are labelled as astrological interpretation.

The transit guide explains the distinction between:

- astronomical planetary positions;
- natal positions;
- angular aspects;
- the interpretive meaning astrologers assign to those relationships.

Oracle Mirror does not fabricate current planetary positions or “today's transits” from static sign stereotypes. A production current-transit feature should use a real ephemeris.

## Birth-chart education

`/astrology/birth-chart` explains the four major interpretive layers:

- planets;
- zodiac signs;
- houses;
- aspects.

It also explains why accurate birth time/location affect the Ascendant and house cusps, and distinguishes tropical from sidereal zodiac frameworks.

## SEO / GAIO

The V2 response layer adds all 43 routes to the sitemap and adds an `Astrology & Lunar Guides` section to `llms.txt`. Standalone pages use canonical URLs and WebApplication, CollectionPage, ItemList, Article and BreadcrumbList structured data where appropriate.

Homepage/realm navigation receives a direct `/astrology` discovery link without moving the new content into the legacy SPA.

## Architecture

- `src/astrology-data.ts` — zodiac, planet, Moon-phase, and retrograde corpus.
- `src/astrology-pages.ts` — route recognition, SSR rendering, SEO/schema, sitemap/LLM discovery, homepage discovery.
- `public/astrology-core.js` — pure local lunar and date helpers.
- `public/astrology.js` — Moon calculator and 15-day window UI.
- `public/astrology.css` — standalone responsive presentation.
- `tests/astrology-lunar.test.ts` — corpus, calculation, route, wording, sitemap, privacy and reduced-motion regressions.

The standalone architecture keeps reference pages outside the legacy monolithic client and loads the Moon client module only on the calculator page.
